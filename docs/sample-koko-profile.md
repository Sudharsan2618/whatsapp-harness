# Koko Exports — business profile

Koko Exports is a coconut and cocoa powder exporter based in Coimbatore, Tamil
Nadu, India, shipping to buyers across Germany and the European Union.

## Products

- Spray-dried coconut milk powder (fat content 50-60%).
- Organic cocoa powder, natural and alkalized (Dutch-processed).
- Desiccated coconut, fine and medium grade.

## Order terms

- Minimum order quantity (MOQ): 500 kg per product.
- Packaging: 25 kg kraft-paper bags with food-grade liner; palletized for export.
- Lead time: 3 to 4 weeks from confirmed order.
- Incoterms: FOB Tuticorin or CIF Hamburg.

## Certifications

- FSSAI licensed.
- EU Organic and USDA Organic certified.
- Phytosanitary certificate issued per shipment.
